Method and limits

  • Fully correct: every required fact present (doses, spacings, intervals, the right action). Unsupported figures: the answer contains a number found in neither the question nor the reference guidance.
  • Off-topic handled: the question was escalated or declined instead of answered.
  • The Q&A set was written by the Isôko team and is a v0.1 baseline, not an independent score. C4IR's 5,000-pair golden dataset is the real benchmark. These results were recorded before any tuning to the test set.
